Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how Loop Hop (圈圈弹), its support service, and this website handle information. You can contact the Loop Hop developer through our Contact page.

Your game stays on your device

Loop Hop does not require an account. Your scores, experience, star coins, characters, outfits, supplies, reward records, saved run, and preferences are stored on your device. We do not upload these game saves to a game server. Your device's backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple.

The core game works offline. The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. If you choose to share a score, your device's share sheet lets you select the destination.

Optional advertising

Live advertising is currently disabled in the standard game. Test versions may offer clearly labelled Google test ads. Choosing a test ad connects to Google even though it does not generate advertising revenue.

Where an advertising feature is available and you choose to use it, Google AdMob and Google's consent service may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. These services use information to deliver advertising, manage privacy choices, measure performance, and prevent abuse. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

Loop Hop requests non-personalized ads and does not request permission to use Apple's advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. We do not add a separate game analytics service. Google's handling, retention, and any international transfers of advertising information are described in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Age and advertising choices

Our live-advertising policy excludes users under 18 and users whose age eligibility cannot be established. On supported iOS versions, the advertising setup can ask Apple for a shared age range. We use the range to decide advertising eligibility; we do not write the range or a birth date into your game save. Declining to share an age range does not block normal play.

When optional live advertising becomes available, applicable privacy choices must be handled before ad requests. The game's advertising settings will allow you to turn off optional ads and, where required, reopen the advertising privacy choices. Turning off ads stops new ad requests; it does not undo information already processed by a provider. Normal play and non-advertising ways to earn rewards remain available.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and resolve problems, with our email service provider processing the correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other information that is unnecessary for support. For an ad complaint, you can include the ad reference shown in the game's privacy screen, if available.

This website

This website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or cookies set by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement. Following an external link brings you to a service governed by its own privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Local progress remains until it is removed from your device. Deleting the app removes its local app data; separate device backups may remain according to your Apple settings. Offloading an app may preserve its data. We cannot recover or delete a local game save through a server account because there is no such account.

We keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Google, GitHub, Apple, and email providers may retain information they process under their own policies. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal information, or to complain to a relevant privacy authority. Use our Contact page about information you have provided to us, or use a provider's privacy controls for information it handles.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes to advertising or data use will also be reflected in the relevant app notices.
